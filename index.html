Nutrient learning guide

Learn the general roles of essential plant nutrients. Symptoms can overlap and are not a reliable diagnosis on their own.

  • N — Nitrogen: supports leafy growth and chlorophyll formation.
  • P — Phosphorus: supports energy transfer, roots and early development.
  • K — Potassium: supports water regulation, enzyme activity and crop quality.
  • S — Sulphur: supports protein formation and several plant processes.

Confirm suspected deficiencies with field assessment and, where possible, soil or plant analysis.

About this project

AgriSmart is a community-built, open-source learning project. Its goal is to make basic farm calculations and agriculture education easier to access.

Important: AgriSmart does not prescribe fertilizer or pesticide doses. Always follow local regulations, product labels, soil-test recommendations and qualified agricultural advice. Check units and calculations before acting.
